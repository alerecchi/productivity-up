# Product

## Purpose

Productivity Up is a todo app that organizes work by time horizon. Todos start broad (someday, this year) and move into narrower horizons (this month, this week, today) as they become more immediate. The app helps people turn long-range intentions into a realistic short list, and it handles unfinished work explicitly instead of letting it pile up.

## Differentiation

Two mechanisms, used together, set the product apart:

- **Time-based Buckets.** There are five Buckets: an Inbox (Someday in the app) that never ends, This Year, This Month, This Week, and Today. Each time-based Bucket covers the current calendar period for its horizon, in the user's timezone.
- **Migration when a Bucket's period ends.** When a Bucket's period ends (for example, today becomes tomorrow), every unfinished todo in it is shown to the user, who decides for each one. They can keep it in the next period of the same horizon (e.g. try again tomorrow) or move it back to the broader Bucket (e.g. this week). Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly Buckets all work this way.

## Users

Knowledge workers and other people who enjoy planning and productivity without being productivity enthusiasts. They plan work and personal life in the same place. Users use the app on both desktop and phone. Desktop may suit long-range planning and the phone may suit today, but users choose how to use each device. The design must not lock a device to one job.

Everything must make sense to someone who has never heard of GTD or productivity systems.

## Brand Personality

- **Energetic.** Upbeat and motivating. It should get people moving, not settle them. Livelier colors, more motion, and more emphasis.
- **Lightly playful.** Mostly plain, with an occasional warm or witty touch. No constant jokes and no cutesiness.
- **Helpful companion.** The app gives a gentle nudge at key moments (a period ends, Today is empty) and then gets out of the way. It doesn't lecture or push the user.
- **Balanced density.** Show the key information at a glance (for example, Category on a todo). Everything else is available on demand.
- **Balanced celebrations.** Scale the celebration to the achievement. For example completing a single todo gets small, quick feedback; clearing Today gets a bigger moment. Celebrations reward finishing work.

### Never feels like

- **Guilt-tripping.** No red "overdue" badges, no shaming unfinished work, no nagging.
- **A corporate project-management tool.** Not dense, not process-heavy, not built for teams and managers.
- **Gamified.** No points, streaks, levels, or progress that is lost when a day is skipped.
- **Hustle culture.** No "crush your goals" pressure. Energy encourages, it never pushes.
- **A generic SaaS template.** It needs a look of its own.
